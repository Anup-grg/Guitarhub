About this preset

Aim for the "record it in one take" feel of Gilmour's outro. The amp sits just on the edge of breakup and almost all of the drama comes from the delay tail, not from gain.

What's inside

  • Amp — a pushed Fender-style clean, bright cap on, presence at 4.
  • Drive — a low-gain booster (Level 6, Tone 5) placed before the amp for the mid hump.
  • Delay — 420 ms modulated delay, mix around 28% on snapshot 2.
  • Cab — two IRs, a Twin-style open-back and an AC-style blues driver cab.

Snapshots

  1. Verse — dry, sit-back clean.
  2. Solo — booster on, delay up, +3 dB output.
  3. Rotary — rotary speaker for the final sustained bends.
Tip: play it with the guitar volume around 8 and ride up for the bends — the clean responds more naturally than the gain does.

How to import

  1. Update to firmware 9.0 and download fm3-edit for your OS.
  2. Connect the FM3 over USB and open FM3-Edit.
  3. Drag comfortably-numb-solo.syx onto the preset grid, or use Preset → Import.
  4. Load the two bundled IRs into cabinet slots User 1 and User 2.
  5. Re-check the Rotary block rate on snapshot 3 and save.
